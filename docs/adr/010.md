# ADR-010: AWS EU managed foundation

Status: Accepted for implementation — 10 September 2026

Source: implementation design v1.0, section 16.2.

## Decision

Use the AWS managed foundation rather than a VPS, following the product owner's decision on 10 September 2026. Maintain eu-west-1 Terraform for ECS, RDS, SQS, KMS, S3, Cognito, SES and observability.

## Consequences

The hosting platform choice is accepted; this is not deployment or production acceptance. Confirm pilot residency, procurement, budget and contractual needs before handling merchant data. Separate accounts and keys per environment. Resource creation, provider qualification, restore drills and independent reviews remain subject to the release gates.
